# minor thoughts on books
I've read a ton of books this year, even by my standards. I suppose part of it is that my social life has take a major step down after my move. I thought it would be fun to download my data from the storygraph and play around with it some. The dataset you download is somewhat less interesting than I thought, it contains no genre marker. I'll combine it with something else aftewards.

I did do a little bit of exploratory analysis on gender for the books I've read this year, largely due to a thread I saw on reddit this week. I found out that I have so far read 64 books by men this year, compared to 34 by women and 1 by a nonbinary author. I also found out that the python package [`gender guesser`](https://pypi.org/project/gender-guesser/) uses `andy` as a synonym for `androgynous`, which both makes sense and is incredibly funny to me personally.

Anyway, I can't believe that in a few hours I'll have read 100 books since the year started, and there's still like 4 Ania Ahlborns and a David Barnette I want to re-read before the month is out.
